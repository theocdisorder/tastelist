Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

TasteList is a place to share the music, movies, TV, books, and podcasts you love. This policy explains what information we collect when you use TasteList at tastelist.app, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email address, used to sign you in and to contact you about your account.
  • Password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see it.
  • Name and profile photo, if you sign in with Google. We receive only your basic profile (name, email, photo). We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.
  • Username, display name, bio, and photo that you set or change on your profile.

What you add to TasteList

  • Your favorites and lists (artists, albums, songs, movies, TV shows, books, podcasts, and anything else you add), plus notes, dates, and links you attach to them.
  • Shared lists you create or join, and the people you invite to them.
  • Follows, follow requests, likes, reactions, and comments.
  • Names of friends you credit as recommending something. These are plain text you type; we don't contact those people.

Information created as you use TasteList

  • Notifications about activity on your profile and lists.
  • Taste Insights: written analysis of connections between your favorites, generated when you view that section of your own profile, and saved so it doesn't need to be regenerated.
  • Artwork and details (covers, posters, summaries) looked up for the things you add.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting providers, such as IP address, browser type, and request times, used for security and troubleshooting.

TasteList does not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To run TasteList: create your account, sign you in, show your profile and lists, and deliver notifications.
  • To show artwork and details for what you add, and to generate Taste Insights when you ask for them.
  • To keep TasteList secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to this policy.

Who can see your information

  • Public profiles: anyone can see your display name, username, photo, bio, favorites, public lists, and public activity.
  • Private profiles: only people whose follow requests you approve can see your favorites and activity. Your name, username, and photo still appear in search and on follow requests.
  • Shared lists: private lists are visible only to you and the people you invite. Public lists can be seen by anyone. Members of a list can see who added each item.
  • Comments and likes are visible to anyone who can see the thing you commented on or liked.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.

Services we rely on

We share information with the following providers only as needed to run TasteList:

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication, file storage, and server functions. Your account and everything you add is stored there.
  • Google provides "Continue with Google" sign-in, if you choose to use it.
  • OpenAI generates Taste Insights. When you request an analysis, the titles, creators, years, genres, and short descriptions of your favorites are sent to OpenAI to write it. Your name and email are not included, and we ask OpenAI not to retain the request for training.
  • Media catalogs: The Movie Database (TMDB), Open Library, Google Books, Apple (iTunes and Apple Podcasts search), and TheAudioDB. When you search for or view items, your browser asks these services for matching titles and artwork. They receive the search terms and, as with any website, your IP address. They do not receive your TasteList account details.
  • Websites you link to: when you add a link, our server visits that page once to fetch its title and preview image.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

Cookies and local storage

TasteList stores your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your favorites, lists, comments, and profile details at any time from within TasteList.
  • Make your profile private so only approved followers can see it, and set each shared list to private or public.
  • Remove followers or leave shared lists.
  • Delete your account by emailing us at the address below. We will delete your account and the content associated with it within 30 days, except where we need to keep limited information to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. Items you added to someone else's shared list may remain on that list without your name attached.
  • Request a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by emailing us.

If you signed in with Google, you can also revoke TasteList's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Server logs kept by our hosting providers are retained for a limited period according to their policies. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information as described above.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to your information in our database is restricted by account-level security rules. No service can guarantee perfect security, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects your account.

Children

TasteList is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, let you know in TasteList or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: tastelist.privacy@gmail.com
